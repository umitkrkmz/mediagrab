Submission options). Kept here so the next submission starts from the last
one. Rules that shaped it (Store Policies 7.20):

- **10.1.1** — the title is just "MediaGrab": no descriptive words, no other
  product's name.
- **10.1.3** — at most 7 search terms, relevant, no prices, no other
  products' names (so no "YouTube" / "yt-dlp" here).
- Site names are kept out of the short description and features too; the
  description says "1700+ sites" and links the open-source engine by URL.
- Every claim must hold for the **Store build**: no in-app yt-dlp updates,
  no Docker install.

## Submission options — restricted capability (runFullTrust, ≤ 500 karakter)

> MediaGrab is a desktop (Win32) app. It runs a local web server (bundled Python) opened in the user's browser, and starts helper programs shipped in the package: ffmpeg to merge audio/video and convert to MP3, and Deno to read media formats. It saves downloads to a user-chosen folder and shows a tray icon. This is not possible in an AppContainer. It installs no drivers or services, never elevates, and runs no code from outside the package.

## English (en-us)

**Short description** (≤ 270 characters)

> Download video and music from 1700+ sites into a tidy personal archive. Pick the quality and format; playlists, subtitles and channel following included. Everything stays on your own PC: no account, no ads, no telemetry.

**Description**

```
MediaGrab downloads video and music from the internet to your own computer and keeps them in a tidy personal archive. It works with 1700+ sites: video platforms, music and podcast services, social media, archive sites.

Paste a link, pick a quality, download. MediaGrab takes care of the rest:

• Video: every available resolution, merged with audio into an MP4.
• Audio: lossless Opus / M4A or MP3 that plays everywhere; cover art and tags written into the file.
• Playlists: open the list and queue any range with one click.
• Subtitles and transcripts: saved under the same name as the video, so players pick them up on their own.
• Channel following: get notified when a channel you follow posts something new, or have it downloaded automatically.
• An organized archive: each download goes into its channel's own folder, with title, source link and description in an info file next to it.
• History and statistics: search, filter by channel and type, see a file's actual resolution and codecs.
• Built-in player: watch or listen without leaving the app.
• Use it from your phone: turn on Remote Access and connect from your phone's browser on the same network with a QR code.
• Safe downloads: a cancelled or failed re-download never touches your existing file, and a download is refused up front if the disk would run out.
• Turkish and English interface, light and dark theme.

Your privacy stays yours: no account, no ads, no telemetry. Your downloads, settings and history stay on your computer. MediaGrab has no server of its own.

Open source: MediaGrab is an open-source project under GPL-3.0, with its source code public on GitHub (github.com/umitkrkmz/mediagrab). The Store version is the same app, installed with one click and updated automatically through the Store. Buying it supports the project.

Please download only content you have the right to download, and respect each site's terms of use.
```
